Cybersecurity

The Cybersecurity degree leverages the department’s expertise in cybersecurity and designation as a Center for Academic Excellence in Cybersecurity Research, providing hands-on training including systems-level thinking and secure coding practices. As a specialization degree, students earning a Bachelors in AI will also earn a Bachelors of Computer Science with no additional coursework required.

The Bachelor of Cybersecurity is accredited by ABET.
