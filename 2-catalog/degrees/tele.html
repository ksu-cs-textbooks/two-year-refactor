Degree Programs

Three degree programs — Computer Science, Cybersecurity, and Artificial Intelligence — share the same two-year computational foundation before specializing entirely in the upper division. See Computing Foundations for that shared core, its design rationale, and the full course-by-course breakdown.

Cybersecurity and Artificial Intelligence are not separate degrees built from scratch — each is the Computer Science degree with a curated choice of upper-division electives. A student completing either specialization earns both that specialization’s degree and a Bachelor of Computer Science, with no additional coursework or cost beyond what the Computer Science degree already requires. A student can instead forgo a named specialization and use that same elective flexibility to chart their own path toward the career they envision.
