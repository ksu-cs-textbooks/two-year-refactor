This chapter lays out the research basis for the redesign, the plan for building it, and how we'll know whether it worked.

- [Theoretical Grounding](./theoretical-grounding) surveys the learning-science and assessment-design literature behind the curriculum's key structural choices — spiral revisiting, communities of practice, evidence-centered assessment design, boundary crossing, and universal design for learning — each page closing with an honest assessment of what the evidence actually supports.
- The [Implementation Timeline](./timeline) lays out the multi-year schedule for building, teaching, and evaluating every course in the two-year core, from initial learning-objective identification through course-by-course creation and post-launch review.
- The [Evaluation Strategy](./evaluation) specifies the validated instruments and administration schedule for measuring whether the redesign actually improves learning outcomes, comparing legacy and redesigned cohorts at the same program milestones rather than assuming the change works.
