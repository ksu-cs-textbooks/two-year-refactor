## Course Description

This course introduces graphs and trees as models for networks and discrete structures. Students use graph terminology, recognize important graph classes, work with matrix and weighted representations, apply traversal and shortest-path algorithms, and analyze trees, spanning trees, planar graphs, Euler paths, and Hamiltonian paths.

## Credit Hours

1 credit

## Course Goals and Learning Outcomes

Measurable Outcomes. Through graded coursework, upon successful completion of this course, students will:

1. Apply proof techniques of direct proof, proof by contradiction, weak and strong induction.
2. Distinguish between inductive and recursive definitions and formulas.
3. Apply recursive definitions and solve basic recurrence relations.
4. Perform computations involving modular arithmetic.
5. Use Euclid's Algorithm and compute the greatest common divisor of two integers.

## Course Prerequisites

Prerequisite: MATH 202
