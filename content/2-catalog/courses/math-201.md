## Course Description

This course develops systematic methods for counting finite configurations. Students work with finite sets and cardinality, apply sum and product rules, use permutations and combinations, and solve problems with binomial coefficients, the Binomial Theorem, the pigeonhole principle, and inclusion-exclusion.

## Credit Hours

1 credit

## Course Goals and Learning Outcomes

Measurable Outcomes. Through graded coursework, upon successful completion of this course, students will:

1. Calculate the size of a finite set and distinguish between finite and infinite sets based on cardinality.
2. Apply counting arguments such as the multiplication principle, inclusion-exclusion principle, and pigeonhole principle.
3. Compute permutations and combinations of a set.
4. Demonstrate knowledge of binomial coefficients and the Binomial Theorem.

## Course Prerequisites

Prerequisite: MATH 200
