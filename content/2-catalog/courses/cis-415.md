## Course Description

A study of the ethical issues raised by computing technologies and the impact on society. Exploration of how one might justify actions in regard to ethical dilemmas within the fields of technology, computer science, information security, and artificial intelligence. Other topics include an examination of terminology used, Profession Codes of Conduct for computer science professionals, ethics of software development, and ethical issues relating to privacy and intellectual property in cyberspace.

## Credit Hours

3 credits

## Course Goals and Learning Outcomes

Upon completion of this course, students will be able to:

1. Encourage students to consider how their knowledge and actions within their profession affect others
2. Expose students to both the ethical and legal issues that are a part of the information technology arena and their effects on business, industry, policy, and society
3. Acquaint students with the ethical responsibilities and codes of conduct expected in the profession
4. Improve students’ ability to think critically and defend decisions logically regarding ethical and moral issues in computing
5. Give students a greater appreciation for alternate points of view
6. Provide students with a minimal theoretical background essential for their understanding of the role that personal values and ethics play in decision-making
7. Recognize professional responsibilities and make informed judgments based on legal principles (ABET 4)
8. Recognize professional responsibilities and make informed judgments based on ethical principles (ABET 4)
9. Evaluate an AI system for issues related to bias, fairness, transparency, accountability, privacy, and responsible use. (ABET 4)

## Course Prerequisites

Prerequisite: CIS 300
