This chapter is the department's course catalog: the shared foundation every degree builds on, the degrees themselves, the individual courses, and the elective lists that give students room to specialize.

- [Computing Foundations](./computing-foundations) describes the two-year core shared identically across every degree, before specialization begins in the upper division.
- [Degree Programs](./degrees) covers the three degrees built on that core — Computer Science, Cybersecurity, and Artificial Intelligence — including how the two specializations relate to the flagship CS degree.
- [Courses](./courses) lists every individual course offered by the department, plus service courses from other departments, ordered by course number.
- [Elective Lists](./elective-lists) gives the curated lists electives are drawn from.
