The ability to confidently and effectively communicate technical ideas to audiences of widely varying backgrounds is a critical skill for every computer scientist. The communication elective provides a safe and encouraging environment for developing these skills. Students must choose one of the following:

- COMM 323 - Nonverbal Communication (3 hours)
- COMM 326 - Group Communication (3 hours)
- MANGT 220 - Principles of Management (3 hours)
- THTRE 171 - Fundamentals of Acting (3 hours)
  - _Note: Taking THTRE 171 also satisfies KSC 060 (core communication requirement)_
- THTRE 265 – Fundamentals of Improvisation (3 hours)
